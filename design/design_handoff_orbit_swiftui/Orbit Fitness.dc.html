1ORBIT — Stronger × MacroFactor, nebula edition

Four screens: Home dashboard, Fuel (diet log), Train (workout logging) and Body (results — the geometric human colored by per-muscle strength level, M / W toggle). Every screen sits on a live outer-space background: drifting star clusters that shift as you scroll, shooting stars, and the occasional passing ship. The WebGL object on Home / Fuel / Train rotates and re-frames on scroll. Interactions are shared across screens: quick-add dinner on Fuel and the rings + macro moons react; tap set circles on Train and the score ticks, the asteroid heats, and trained muscles light up on Body.
